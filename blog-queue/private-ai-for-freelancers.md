## Why do freelancers and small businesses worry about AI privacy?

Because the data is not yours to share. When a client hands you a brief, a contract, or their customers' details, they are trusting you to handle it, and feeding it into a cloud AI uploads it to a company that was never part of that agreement. For regulated work, NDA work, or anything with personal data in it, that can breach the very obligations you are paid to keep. The general case for keeping confidential material off the cloud is in [offline AI for sensitive work](/blog/offline-ai-for-sensitive-work).

A local assistant sidesteps the whole issue. If the AI runs on your machine and the client's brief never leaves it, there is no third party to account for and no upload to disclose. You get the help without taking on the risk.

## Can I use a local AI assistant for client work?

Yes, and the privacy is the reason it suits client work specifically. Because InnerZero processes everything on your own hardware and stores its memory locally, the sensitive material in a client project stays on your computer. You can draft, summarise, and research against real client context without that context being sent anywhere. The local-first idea behind that is explained in [what is a local AI assistant](/what-is-local-ai).

It also remembers across conversations, so it can hold the context of an ongoing client or project without you re-pasting the brief every time. That makes it feel less like a generic chatbot and more like an assistant that knows your work.

## What can it help a small business with?

The everyday admin and drafting that eats a freelancer's week. Common uses:

- Drafting and tidying client emails, proposals, and project updates.
- Summarising long documents, briefs, or notes into something quick to act on.
- Researching a topic or market, then pulling the findings together.
- Keeping separate clients or projects in their own space so context does not bleed.
- Handling calendar and mail through opt-in connectors so scheduling and follow-ups are less manual.

None of that requires sending client data to a cloud service. For a fuller tour of what the app does out of the box, see [5 things you can do right now](/blog/things-you-can-do-with-innerzero).

## Does it keep client data private?

Yes, by design. The AI runs on your machine, memory is stored in a local database, and nothing is uploaded by default. Any cloud feature is opt-in and off unless you turn it on, and even then only the specific prompt you send goes out, not your whole memory or files. The full privacy posture is in [how InnerZero stays private](/blog/how-innerzero-stays-private).

For client work, that default is the selling point. You can tell a client, accurately, that their brief was processed on your own computer and not uploaded to a third-party AI service, which is a sentence most cloud-tool users cannot say.

## How does it compare to a per-seat cloud AI subscription?

The cost shape is different, and so is the privacy. Per-seat cloud AI tools charge a recurring fee for every user and run everything on their servers, so your costs scale with headcount and your client data leaves your machines. InnerZero's local app is free for personal use and runs on hardware you already own, and for commercial use the Business Licence is a flat per-seat permission rather than metered compute. If you want to avoid recurring software costs as much as possible, the approach in [AI without a subscription](/blog/ai-without-subscription) applies here too.

The bigger difference for a small business is where the data sits. A cloud subscription means trusting a vendor with your clients' information. A local assistant keeps it on your own machines, which is often the easier story to tell a client and the safer one to stand behind.

### Is InnerZero free for business use?

The app is free for personal use, education, and non-profits. Commercial or client work needs a Business Licence, currently £19.99 a month or £149.99 a year per seat (£129.99 a year per seat from five seats).

### Can it keep different clients separate?

Yes. You can keep separate areas of memory, so one client's context does not bleed into another's.

### Is the Business Licence different from InnerZero Pro?

Yes. The Business Licence is commercial-use permission for the same app. InnerZero Pro is a separate optional membership for local extras like themes and personalities.

### Do I need an internet connection to use it for work?

No. Once set up, the assistant runs offline on your own hardware, so client work does not depend on a connection or a cloud service.
