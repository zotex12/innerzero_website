The default voice of most AI assistants is the same beige helpfulness: polite, a bit wordy, weirdly eager. It is fine, but it is nobody's favourite. InnerZero lets you change how your assistant speaks, from crisp and professional to warm and casual, or a style you write yourself. It is a small setting that changes how the whole thing feels to use.

> **Quick summary**
> - InnerZero lets you set a personality that shapes how your assistant speaks in every reply.
> - It ships with built-in options like Professional, Friendly, and Concise, and you can write your own.
> - Personality is separate from the voice and from the assistant's name. It only changes tone.
> - It is off by default, so you choose if and when to give it a character.

## Can I change my AI assistant's personality?

Yes. InnerZero has a personality setting that shapes the tone and style of how the assistant talks. Turn one on and it applies to every reply, in text and in voice, so the change is consistent rather than something you have to ask for each time. It is off by default, so out of the box you get a neutral assistant until you decide to give it a character.

This is more than a gimmick. The tone an assistant uses affects how comfortable it is to talk to all day. A clipped, no-nonsense style suits some people and some tasks, and a warmer, chattier one suits others.

## What personalities come built in?

InnerZero ships with a set of ready-made personalities so you can pick a feel without writing anything. They range from a formal Professional tone to a warm Friendly one to a stripped-back Concise style that keeps answers short, along with more characterful options like a Coach, a Mentor, an Executive style, a Storyteller, and a couple of playful ones. Some of the built-in personalities are part of InnerZero Pro, while others are free to use, and you can always create your own at no cost.

The quickest way to find your fit is to try a couple. The same question answered by the Concise style and by the Friendly style reads very differently, and you will know within a few replies which one you want to keep.

## How do I create my own personality?

You write a short instruction describing how you want the assistant to speak, give it a name, and switch it on. Something like "reply in clear British English, be direct, skip the preamble, and never use emoji" becomes a personality the assistant follows in every response. It is plain language, not code, so you describe the style the way you would describe it to a person.

That custom option is where it gets genuinely useful. You can dial in exactly the tone you like: how formal, how brief, how much it explains, whether it pushes back or just agrees. For the broader set of things you can adjust, see [how to customise InnerZero](/blog/customise-innerzero).

## Does the personality change the voice or the assistant's name?

No, and that separation is deliberate. Personality controls the writing style and tone. It is kept apart from which [voice](/blog/voice-mode-innerzero) reads replies aloud and from the assistant's own identity, so you can mix them freely. You might pair a concise written style with a calm voice, or a friendly style with a brighter one, without one setting forcing the other.

Keeping these separate means you can tune each part of the experience on its own. The way it sounds, the way it writes, and what you call it are three dials, not one.

## Will it remember my personality choice?

Yes. Once you set a personality, it stays active across conversations until you change it, so you are not re-selecting it every session. Because the whole app runs locally, that preference lives on your own machine like the rest of your settings, with nothing about it uploaded. The local-first idea behind that is in [what is a local AI assistant](/what-is-local-ai).

You can switch at any time. Many people keep a default they like and flip to a different style for a specific job, such as a concise tone for quick work questions and a warmer one for casual chat.

## Which personality should I pick?

Start with the task you do most. A few rough fits:

- **Professional or Concise** for work, quick answers, and getting to the point.
- **Friendly** for casual everyday chat and a warmer feel.
- **Coach or Mentor** when you want encouragement and a bit of guidance in the tone.
- **A custom one** when none of the presets is quite your style and you want it exact.

There is no wrong answer, and you are not locked in. The best approach is to try one for a day, notice whether it makes the assistant nicer to talk to, and adjust. Tone is personal, which is the whole reason it is a setting and not a fixed default.

If you are not sure where to start, the custom route is often the most satisfying. Write down, in a sentence or two, how you wish the assistant talked to you, the things that annoy you about a default AI voice, and the things you want more of. Set that as your personality and use it for a week. You can refine the wording any time, so it tends to get sharper the more you live with it, ending up as a tone that genuinely fits the way you work rather than a preset that almost does.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the personality on by default?

No. Personality is off by default, so you get a neutral assistant until you choose to turn one on.

### Can I write my own personality?

Yes. You describe the style you want in plain language, name it, and switch it on. You can create your own at no cost.

### Are all the built-in personalities free?

Some built-in personalities are free and some are part of InnerZero Pro. Writing your own custom personality is free either way.

### Does personality change the assistant's voice?

No. Personality controls writing tone and is kept separate from voice selection and from the assistant's identity, so you can combine them however you like.

### Does my personality choice sync to a server?

No. It is stored locally on your machine like the rest of your settings, with nothing uploaded.
