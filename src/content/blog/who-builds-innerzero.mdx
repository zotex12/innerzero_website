## Who makes InnerZero?

InnerZero is built by Summers Solutions, a UK digital studio based in Birmingham. It is a registered company in England and Wales, and it is founder-led, which means the person designing the product is the same person standing behind it.

I am Louie Summers, the founder. I started Summers Solutions to build software that respects the people using it, and InnerZero is the clearest expression of that idea: a private AI assistant that runs on your own computer instead of someone else's servers. There is no faceless team and no investor steering the product away from its privacy promise. If you want the longer story, [why we built InnerZero](/blog/why-we-built-innerzero) covers it.

## Why does it matter who builds your AI assistant?

It matters because a personal AI assistant handles some of your most sensitive information: your questions, your work, your worries, your half-formed ideas. The company behind it decides what happens to all of that. With most cloud AI, the honest answer is that your data helps the provider, not just you. Read the terms of a typical cloud assistant and you will usually find broad permission to store, analyse, and train on what you type. That is not a scandal, it is the business model. It is just rarely said out loud.

A maker's incentives show up in the product. A company that earns money from your attention or your data will design for that. A company that earns money by selling you a tool you own will design for you. InnerZero is the second kind. The business model is stated plainly on the [pricing page](/pricing): the local app is free to download, and the optional extras are exactly that, optional.

## Can you trust a small studio with your AI?

A small studio is not a weakness here, it is the point. Big platforms answer to shareholders and advertisers, and your data is often part of how they pay the bills. A founder-led studio answers to the people who use its software, because that is where the money comes from. Smaller also means accountable. When a decision needs explaining or something needs fixing, you reach a person, not a support queue that loops you in circles. InnerZero is shaped by that closeness. Every privacy decision in the app was made by someone who has to stand behind it in public.

## Is InnerZero still private if a company is behind it?

Yes, and this is the part that surprises people. InnerZero is private because of how it is built, not because of a promise written by its maker. The AI runs on your hardware. Your [memory](/blog/ai-that-remembers) lives in a local database on your own drive. The desktop app sends no telemetry and phones nothing home.

That means the company behind InnerZero genuinely cannot see your conversations. There is no server holding your chats, so there is nothing for us to read, sell, or lose in a breach. If you want the technical detail, [how InnerZero stays private](/blog/how-innerzero-stays-private) walks through exactly what does and does not leave your machine. Privacy you can verify beats privacy you have to trust.
